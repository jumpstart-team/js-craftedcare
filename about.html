Dr. Wesp is a mother first. She built Crafted Care for more time with every family: to answer every question, know your child's story, and be a real partner as your family grows.

Before founding Crafted Care, she saw firsthand how the traditional seven-minute visit leaves parents with more questions than answers. Crafted Care is her answer: a pediatric practice built around unhurried time, direct access, and a real relationship with the same doctor from your baby's first days through their teenage years.

In plain English
What is direct primary care, really?

Direct primary care means your family pays one simple monthly membership instead of billing insurance for every visit. That's what makes the rest possible: longer appointments, direct access to Dr. Wesp, and same-day or next-day availability when something comes up.

You'll still use your insurance the way you always have, for labs, imaging, specialists, and prescriptions. What changes is your everyday pediatric care. No rushing through your questions. No repeating your child's whole history at every visit. No waiting weeks to be seen.

It's the kind of care Dr. Wesp always wished she had, for her own kids and now for yours.
