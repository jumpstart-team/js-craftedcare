"I am also a mother and a patient myself, and I believe the best care comes from a real relationship, built with the time to make one."
Krystyna Wesp, DO, FAAP
Founder

Dr. Wesp is a mother first. She is also an osteopathic physician and a Fellow of the American Academy of Pediatrics. She built Crafted Care to care for the whole child and the whole family together. That means a pediatrician who knows your child's story from day one.

When visits are billed through insurance, the schedule tends to set the pace, and families often see a different provider each time. Crafted Care runs on one simple monthly membership instead. That lets Dr. Wesp be the pediatrician your family sees at every visit, with easy access and time for every question, from your baby's first days into adulthood.

What is direct primary care, really?
