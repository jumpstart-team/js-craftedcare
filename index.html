Homepage options, for review
Three distinct directions for the Crafted Care Pediatrics homepage, built against the real brand kit. Nothing here is live or final — pick the overall direction that feels right and we'll build it out for real.
Option A
The Open Book
A normal, fully scrolling and navigable homepage, with the hero itself styled as a literal open book — two pages, a center spine, dog-eared corners. The book motif lives in the hero, not the whole site's navigation.
Option B
Bold Editorial
A confident, modern break from the crafted-book motif: oversized type, high contrast, an asymmetric layout. Same brand colors, pushed punchier — no chapters, no book graphics.
Option C
Full Conversion Build
The complete single-scroll homepage: problem, solution, benefits, pricing, about, and an FAQ, framed as chapters throughout. Best for a parent doing real research before joining the waitlist.
